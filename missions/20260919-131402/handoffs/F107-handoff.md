## Notes for the next worker
- The `react-hooks/refs` and `react-hooks/set-state-in-effect` ESLint rules are new/strict in this repo's current eslint-plugin-react-hooks config — if you're writing new code-editor components, watch for: (1) never assign to `ref.current` in the render body (do it in a `useEffect`), (2) prefer a lazy `useState(() => ...)` initializer over `useEffect(() => setState(...), [])` for "read once on mount" patterns.
