## Decisions made
- **Replaced `WaitingOnYouBlock` outright rather than adding the callout alongside it.** That block (built from `buildWaitingOnYouItems`, `lib/portal/build-waiting-on-you-items.ts`) listed individual approval/task/deliverable/account/brief rows — the exact rows F006's "For you" page now lists in full. Per the clarified spec ("replace it with the callout rather than showing both"), the callout is the one entry point into that detail now. `buildWaitingOnYouItems`/`WaitingOnYouBlock` themselves are left in place (still independently unit-tested, `build-waiting-on-you-items.test.ts` untouched and still green) rather than deleted — no other call site references them, flagged below for a future cleanup pass.
- **Dropped `accountsResult`/`briefResult` (and their underlying `getClientVisiblePortalAccounts`/`getBriefForClient` reads) from this page** — they existed ONLY to feed `buildWaitingOnYouItems`'s two extra categories (pending client-owned accounts, a draft brief) that `getWaitingOnYouCount` (F005, decisions+materials only) deliberately does not cover. Once the block they fed is gone, those two reads/results were fully dead code; removing them keeps the page free of unused fetches rather than fetching data nothing renders. This means a client-owned pending account or a draft-with-questions brief no longer surfaces as its own row on Home — both are still reachable from their own pages (Your site / Questionnaire), just not flagged on the Home callout. Flagged as an explicit, deliberate scope narrowing per the clarified spec's own instruction, not an oversight.
- **Callout uses the design system `Card` primitive directly** (`shadow-xs` and the elevation/border/radius tokens are already baked into `components/ui/card.tsx`) rather than a hand-rolled bordered `div`, per CLAUDE.md's "Cards carry `shadow-xs`" rule.
- **"Review now" is a plain `<Link>` styled with `buttonVariants({ variant: "primary", size: "sm" })`, not `<Button asChild>`** — this codebase's `Button` wraps `@base-ui/react/button`'s primitive, which has no `asChild` prop (checked directly); every other Link-styled-as-a-button in this codebase (e.g. `ApprovalCard`'s own "Open" link) already follows the "style the anchor with the button's own class function" pattern rather than nesting elements, so this follows that existing convention instead of introducing a new one.
- Count renders `total` as its own `<span className="font-mono">` (design system: "Data is mono... counts") separate from the surrounding sans-serif sentence.
